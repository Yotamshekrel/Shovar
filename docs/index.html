How it works

From crumpled receipt to money spent.

Four steps, and Shovar does most of the work.

  1. Photograph the credit note, gift card or screenshot. Shovar reads the store, amount, currency, dates and code for you.

  2. Anything Shovar isn’t sure about is highlighted. Fix it with a tap and save. The code is encrypted on your phone.

  3. Seriously. Shovar reminds you 14 and 3 days before it expires, and when you’re near a store where it works.

  4. At the till, show the barcode, QR or the code in big text. Used part of it? Log it, and the balance updates.

    Is Shovar free?

    Yes. Shovar is free to download and has no ads.

    Do I need to sign up?

    No. There’s no account and nothing to log in to. Open the app and add your first card.

    Where is my data stored?

    Only on your phone. Codes and PINs are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key kept in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore, and you can lock the app with Face ID or fingerprint. We don’t run servers that hold your wallet, and there are no analytics.

    How does reading a receipt work?

    AI reading is optional. The first time, Shovar asks for your permission and explains that only the image you chose is sent, only to pull out the details. You can turn it off in Settings and type cards in by hand any time.

    Does it track my location?

    Nearby reminders are off until you turn them on. They use your phone’s low-power geofencing, and your exact position never leaves the device. To find branches, Shovar only shares store names and a rough area, rounded to about 10 km.

    Which gift cards does it know?

    Any store credit, return note or voucher works. For multi-store cards, Shovar knows where BuyMe (ALL, Total, Style, Brunch), Tav Zahav, Max GiftCard (including Super, Executive and Food), Dream Card and Nofshonit are accepted.

    Is it in Hebrew?

    כן! The whole app works in Hebrew and English, with a proper right-to-left layout, and search understands both spellings of a store’s name.
